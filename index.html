FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does RefereeX AI replace referees?

No. RefereeX AI supports human officials with data, replays, and confidence scores. Final decisions remain with the referee and VAR team.

How many cameras are supported?

The platform supports 16+ synchronized camera inputs including broadcast, tactical, goal-line, and drone feeds.

What is the latency?

Edge deployments achieve sub-100ms incident detection. Cloud fusion adds typically 200–500ms for full multi-camera assessment.

Can it integrate with existing VAR?

Yes. RESTful APIs and standard video protocols enable integration with existing VAR infrastructure and broadcast systems.
